# Privacy Policy for CWT - Can's WhatsApp Tool

**Last Updated: February 8, 2026**

## Overview

CWT (Can's WhatsApp Tool) is a Chrome browser extension that exports WhatsApp Web conversations to local backup files. This privacy policy explains how the extension handles your data.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any data.**

CWT does not:
- Collect personal information
- Track your browsing activity
- Store your WhatsApp messages on external servers
- Send any data to third-party services
- Use analytics or telemetry
- Create user accounts or profiles

## How the Extension Works

1. When you use CWT, the extension reads your WhatsApp Web chat data directly from your browser
2. All processing happens **locally** on your device
3. Exported files are saved **only** to your local computer
4. No data ever leaves your browser or is transmitted over the internet

## Permissions Used

The extension requires the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| `activeTab` | Access the WhatsApp Web tab when you click the extension icon |
| `scripting` | Inject export functionality into WhatsApp Web page |
| `host_permissions` | Limited to `web.whatsapp.com` only - to interact with WhatsApp Web |

These permissions are the minimum required for the extension to function. They are **only** used when you actively interact with the extension.

## Third-Party Services

CWT does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services.

## Data Storage

- CWT does not store any data
- Your exported files are saved locally on your device
- No cloud storage or sync features are used

## Children's Privacy

CWT does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.
